The difference

Why clients stay.

  1. 01

    Texture-first consultations

    We look at your hair before we talk about styles. Porosity, density and what it has been through all shape the plan.

  2. 02

    We sell what we install

    Every bundle and unit in the shop is hair we have cut, coloured and pressed ourselves. If it does not hold up on the chair, it does not go on the shelf.

  3. 03

    Aftercare you can actually do

    You leave knowing how to wrap it, wash it and sleep on it, with a written routine texted to you the same evening.
